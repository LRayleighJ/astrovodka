astrovodka.L_coords — Angular Momentum Coordinates

A NamedTuple specifying source orientation based on angular momentum alignment.

L_coords(incl=0 deg, pa=270 deg, az_rot=0 deg)

Parameters:
NameTypeDescription
inclQuantity[deg]Inclination angle. 0° = face-on, 90° = edge-on (default: 0).
paQuantity[deg]Position angle on sky, East of North, receding side (default: 270).
az_rotQuantity[deg]Azimuthal rotation around angular momentum axis (default: 0).
Returns: L_coords NamedTuple
  • Algorithm: The angular momentum vector of the central 1/3 of particles (HI-mass-weighted) is aligned to the x-axis (face-on). Then az_rot rotates around L, incl tilts the disc toward/away from LOS (y-axis rotation), and pa sets the sky position angle (second x-axis rotation).
  • All rotations are right-handed and extrinsic.
  • Usage: source.rotate(L_coords=L_coords(incl=60*U.deg, pa=270*U.deg))
